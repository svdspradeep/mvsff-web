Connecting Chettiars Globally

Global KKDI

About Global KKDI

In the old days, Chettiars were primarily settled in Karaikudi and the surrounding villages (Chettinadu). With time, due to various factors, the Chettiar community has become widely disseminated throughout the world.

Despite this dispersion, Chettiars have managed to maintain their roots. They have congregated in multiple cities throughout the world, in order to cultivate the Chettiar culture and values. Still, the physical distance between the members of the Chettiar community is a major hindrance to further development of the community.

Bridging the Geographic Distance

Global KKDI is the idea of having a virtual community center for all Chettiars throughout the world. The Meyyappa and Valliammai Subramaniyam Family Foundation regards this concept to be crucial for the survival of the Chettiar community and culture, and will strive to develop it, particularly from the standpoint of youth involvement.

The Meyyappa and Valliammai Subramaniyam Family Foundation may support these initiatives through direct sponsorship and / or via other suitable organizations.

Meyyappa and Valliammai Subramaniyam Family Foundation

Serving the Chettiar community through custom heritage programs, Tamil literary projects, global connection networks, and lung disease pulmonology research.
