Core Vision & Pillars

Our Mission

Chettinad Culture and Values

Chettinad culture refers to the distinctive cultural heritage of the Chettinad region in the southern part of Tamil Nadu, India. It is characterized by a unique blend of traditions, architecture, cuisine, and art forms that reflect the rich history and heritage of the Chettiar community.

Chettiars, also known as Nattukottai Chettiars, are a prominent business community originating from the Chettinad region of Tamil Nadu, India. Their values are deeply rooted in their cultural and traditional practices, reflecting their unique identity and ethos.

Tamil Language

Promoting the Tamil language is not just a matter of linguistic preservation; it's a celebration of cultural diversity, a preservation of heritage, and an investment in the future of knowledge and identity.

South Indian Arts

Chettinad has a rich tradition of music and dance, with classical forms like Bharatanatyam and Carnatic music being highly revered. Traditional folk arts like Karagattam and Kavadi Aattam are also performed during festivals.

Chettinad Cuisine

Famous for rich and flavorful dishes, utilizing aromatic spices and freshly ground masalas. Vegetarian specialties like kara kuzhambu and kothu parotta are popular, known for bold flavors served on banana leaves.

Chettiar Youth

We intend to encourage Chettiar youth to explore professions that are under-represented in our community, including: Law, Politics, Performing Arts, and Professional Creative tracks.

Medical Research Focus

By supporting medical investigations and research specifically focused on lung diseases, we can help uncover new insights into the underlying causes and mechanisms of these conditions. This support can drive the development of cutting-edge therapies, improve early detection methods, and enhance patient care and outcomes.

Investing in lung disease research means empowering scientists, clinicians, and healthcare professionals to explore new frontiers in medicine. It allows for collaborative efforts that bring together the brightest minds from across the globe, working towards a common goal: to breathe new life into those affected by lung diseases.

Meyyappa and Valliammai Subramaniyam Family Foundation

Serving the Chettiar community through custom heritage programs, Tamil literary projects, global connection networks, and lung disease pulmonology research.
